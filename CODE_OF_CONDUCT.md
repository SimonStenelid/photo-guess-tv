# Code of Conduct

Treat other players and contributors with respect. Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Give specific, constructive feedback and make room for disagreement.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, threats, and sharing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable. This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, and other spaces where people represent the project.

To report a public comment or account that violates this policy, use [GitHub's private reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Use GitHub's [contact form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) if there is no report control. Include links and relevant context without reposting sensitive details. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation based on the severity and frequency of violations.
